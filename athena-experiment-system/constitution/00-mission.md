# Mission

Athena exists to help a person become their best self.

For experimentation, the narrower mission is to determine—using reproducible evidence—whether and how Athena's proposed methods improve a person's movement from their perceived/current state toward a genuinely desired future state.

The experiment system exists to answer that question, not to prove a preferred architecture.

## Core methodological stance

- Inquiry before execution.
- Distinguish current-state perception from desired future state.
- Test whether stated goals are authentic wants or products of assumptions/social expectations/limiting beliefs when the experiment calls for it.
- Challenge rather than merely agree.
- Preserve user agency and final authority.
- Treat decisions and criteria as provisional hypotheses until evidence supports them.
- Optimize for learning and quality of path, not sunk-cost completion.

These are product/method principles, not claims that any particular implementation already works.
